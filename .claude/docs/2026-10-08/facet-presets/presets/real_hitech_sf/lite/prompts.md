## PROBE_ANCHORS
Physically based 3D render, photographic lens perspective from a slightly raised viewpoint. No outlines or interior lines; surfaces and light alone define form. Continuous gradient shading, soft form shadows on one to two planes, soft contact shadows; in daylight scenes mostly dark planes, night scenes keep the same palette roles, only darker. Palette: #7b7f85 mid gray about 50%, #2c3442 deep blue-gray about 20%, #1fa5e6 cyan accent ≤ 5% as rim light, low saturation, darks tinted blue, not pure black. Simple round masses, smooth curves. Polished finish, no pattern fills. Soft vertical wall panels, unless the scene specifies its own backdrop. Detail budget: level 3, about 6 main objects.

## PRIORITY
Priority order: low-key blue-gray palette → material contrast → small cyan rim light → photographic perspective with shallow depth → lineless gradient shading.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid flat vector illustration, anime background art, generic stock product photo, clay-style 3D icon, black outlines, flat cel shading, bright high-key lighting, extra props, lens flares, decorative surface patterns, oversharpening, excessive grain, banding, readable text, logos, no speed lines.
