## CAPSULE
Use digital ink linework and cel fills with continuous shading. Throughout, #080703 closed outlines are 0.254% of image width, 2.6 px at 1024 px width; structural lines are 0.146%, 1.5 px at 1024 px width, with rounded joins and no hatching. Keep flat fills with value gradients confined to broad planes. Daylight luminance is 57% to 66%, with near black under 2%, not dominant; nights keep palette roles and shallow shading, only darker. #fadf81 is backdrop only, never object planes; bodies keep assigned colors and #fdf9e7 lit planes; saturated accent #c72125 ≤ 9.4% of the image, confined to broad flexible planes and absent from transparent or neutral reflections. Detail budget: minimal; about 20 structural lines per figure, one hair mass without strand lines, about three eye elements, no decorative marks.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid modern anime, chibi, semi realistic portraiture, and 3D animation; moire, photographic glare, or compression ringing; tiny realistic eyes, elongated fashion proportions, thick sticker borders, or oversized platform shoes; copied still life arrangements, window layouts, or outfit combinations; text, logos, watermarks, intricate iris rings, hair strand engraving, skin pores, wet floor reflections, and speed lines.

## SCENE
Create two square scenes. First, six still life objects rest on an indoor table beside a window: a sphere partly overlapped by draped cloth, a metal cup and glass, and fruit by a leafy potted plant, seen slightly from above. Second, two casually dressed figures stand side by side, facing forward and fully visible against a plain backdrop and floor.

## PROBE_ANCHORS

Major forms occupy 21.9% to 50.8% of frame height; preserve open upper center and separate silhouettes on broad contact surfaces without ground shadows. Detail budget: minimal; use about 20 structural lines per figure, no hair strand lines, about three eye elements, and no decorative marks.
