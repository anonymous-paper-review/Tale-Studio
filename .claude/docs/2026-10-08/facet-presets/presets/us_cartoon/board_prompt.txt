Any figures are drawn in flat television-cartoon proportions, roughly five and a half heads tall, with simplified tubular limbs and small simple hands; large round white eyes with a small dark round pupil and a thin dark upper lid line, no visible iris coloring and no eyelashes; a small simple curved nose with no nostrils and a simple mouth drawn as one outlined shape; hair reads as one solid flat color mass with a few pointed clumps at the silhouette; skin and garments are filled with flat unmodulated color, shaded with at most one darker step, and every form is bounded by a heavy black contour of even weight.
