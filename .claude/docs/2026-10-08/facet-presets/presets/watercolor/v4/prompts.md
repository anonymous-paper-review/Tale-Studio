## CAPSULE
Digital painting with a watercolor appearance: transparent layered washes, pigment pooling at dry edges and granulating in paper grain, light ground showing. Lines appear only on some metal and glass edges, about 0.2% of image width (2 px at 1024 px width), in darker local colour such as #7a7564, swelling then dissolving into wash; elsewhere colour edges, no hatching. Uneven continuous washes model volume, one connected soft shadow per form, no cel steps; metal keeps broken vertical reflections, glass thin bright arcs, no specular dots on cloth or matte forms. In daylight scenes mean luminance about 57%, near-black about 2%, never broad pure black; soft contact shadows #615545 fade lower right about 70% of object height. Objects are muted earth bodies in #a28865 and #453726 darks with #e1d8c0 lit planes, sharing #cfc3a6 wall tone; saturated reddish brown accent #8e4c22 ≤ 3% of the image, two large pieces on round forms and metal reflections, never broad wall or cloth. Detail budget: medium — about 27 garment lines and 40 short hem grooves per figure, hair one mass, no strand lines, no decorative marks.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid anime, cartoon, photorealism and oil painting looks, uniform black outlines, flat cel shading, compressed torsos, oversized hands and feet, copied still life arrangements, copied figure lineups, outfits or hairstyles, bokeh fields, geometric hatching, intricate iris filaments, wet floor reflections, speed lines, readable text, logos and watermarks.
