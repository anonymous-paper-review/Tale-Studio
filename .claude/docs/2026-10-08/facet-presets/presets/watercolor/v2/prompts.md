## CAPSULE
Digital painting with a watercolor appearance uses transparent washes, grain, paper reserves, dry edges, and pigment pooling. Broken boundaries, including #7a7564, dissolve, measure 0.10–0.39% of width (2–4 px at 1024–2048 px), and use no black, hatching, or secondary contour. Continuous washes model curves and reflections with one soft connected rounded or wedged shadow and broken highlights. In daylight, target mean luminance 57%, near black ≤2%, never dominant, and light planes 12.5%; cast #615545 soft contact shadows about 70% of object height, with 1.17% width transitions. Use low saturation warm #cfc3a6, #a28865, #453726, and #e1d8c0; no hue is ground only, while high saturation accent #8e4c22 ≤ 3% of the image forms about two large rounded or narrow vertical pieces, never broad walls or cloth. Diffuse upper left light and slightly high linear perspective ground forms without pattern fill or bloom unless the scene specifies its own backdrop. Detail budget: medium — about 27 structural lines per figure regardless of size, one hair mass with no strand lines and about eight tips, about six eye elements, and no decorative marks.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid anime, cartoon, photorealism, and oil painting; uniform black outlines, flat cel shading, compressed torsos, oversized hands or feet, copied still life arrangements, copied figure lineups, outfits or hairstyles, readable text, logos, watermarks, bokeh fields, geometric hatching, intricate iris filaments, wet floor reflections, decorative marks, and speed lines.
