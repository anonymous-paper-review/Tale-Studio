## PROBE_ANCHORS
Digital transparent watercolor wash with bare paper, in linear perspective from a low overhead viewpoint. No outlines and no interior lines, forms separate by color shifts. Painterly gradient fill, about four to five value steps, soft form shadows as gentle crescents on one or two planes, soft contact cast shadows. Palette: cream #d8cdb6 primary, tan #b99a70 secondary, red-orange #d9482f accent ≤ 5%, low to medium saturation, warm gray-brown darks. Round smooth forms, irregular breaks only in cloth and leaves. Cold-press paper grain with pigment pooling, no pattern fills. Soft blurred background washes, unless the scene specifies its own backdrop. Detail budget: level 3, about six main objects.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid gouache, ink-and-wash line art, photorealistic still life, black outlines, flat cel fills, hard drop shadows, extra hatching, fine leaf veins, extra props, photographic noise, readable text or logos, no speed lines.
