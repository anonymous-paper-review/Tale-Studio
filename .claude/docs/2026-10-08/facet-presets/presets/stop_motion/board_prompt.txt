STYLE REFERENCE — the FIRST reference image sets the visual style ONLY: match its art medium, rendering technique, linework, shading, lighting mood and color grade exactly. Do NOT reproduce its subject or objects.
Everything is rendered as handmade stop-motion photography of a physical tabletop miniature set. Any figures are handmade physical puppets built from needle-felted wool and sculpted clay over an armature - matte felted-fibre skin, wool-roving hair, real miniature knitted and felted fabric with visible fibre, seams and fraying, and small glossy bead eyes set into the sculpted face. Not a drawing, and not a live-action photograph of real people.
Character design reference board: two entirely original adults standing side by side, full body from the top of the head to the shoes, facing the viewer, relaxed neutral faces with open eyes looking straight ahead, arms hanging loosely at their sides, feet slightly apart. Left: a young woman with chin-length dark brown hair, a plain light grey short-sleeve t-shirt, dark navy trousers and white sneakers. Right: a young man with short black hair, a plain olive green crew-neck sweater, dark blue jeans and brown leather shoes. The two figures fill most of the frame height and nothing overlaps them. Plain pale backdrop and a flat uniform floor, soft even front light, no props, no furniture, no patterns, no shadows cast by anything but the two figures.
No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark. 1:1.
