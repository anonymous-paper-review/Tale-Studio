## CAPSULE
Stop-motion craft look: handmade models photographed with a real lens, rough molded surfaces and fuzzy fabric beside true metal and glass optics. No drawn outlines; forms separate by material change, continuous wrapping shading and contact shadow; interior marks are only shaded grooves and seams about 0.1% of image width, no hatching. Shapes are spheres, gentle cones and thick oval plates with thick rounded ends; no cel steps, one soft shadow per rounded form, soft contact cast shadows tinted by the surface below, never flat ellipses. In daylight, mean luminance about 40%, near-black about 16%, not mostly black, about 10% bright planes; night keeps these palette roles, only darker. Warm browns #6d5641 and #b39c81, shadows #3a2717 and #191512, lit planes #d1bd9e; objects keep their material colors; muted accent #2f3743 and #773720 ≤ 11% of the image, few large planes on fabric and fruit, never walls or metal rims, no neon. Warm soft light from the upper left, slight high angle, blurred background, matte rough walls and wood, no bloom. Detail budget: dense — about 18 structural lines per clothed figure plus about 8 shoelace bars, hair one connected fiber mass, eyes three elements, no decorative marks.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid cel animation, photorealistic portraiture, glossy CGI cartoon, flat vector illustration, black ink outlines, smooth plastic skin, elongated fashion proportions, flat cel shading, copied still-life arrangement, character pairing, outfits or hairstyles, intricate iris patterns, individual eyelashes, ornamental costume trim, wet reflective floors, readable text, logos, watermarks, moire, compression blocks, color fringing, speed lines.
