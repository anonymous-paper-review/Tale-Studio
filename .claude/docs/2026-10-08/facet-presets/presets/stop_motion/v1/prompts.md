## CAPSULE
Photographed stop motion craft models use pressed matte fibres for cloth, plants, and fruit; metal reflects, glass refracts, without outlines. Detail budget: sparse, about four boundaries, about six grooves, no hatching. Continuous gradients make one connected soft shadow; sharp highlights stay on metal and glass. Soft #1e1207 contact shadows extend down right about 38.5% of object height, blurred roughly 1.17%. Daylight averages 39.97% luminance, 16.10% nondominant near black, and 9.5% bright planes; night preserves roles darker. Use #6d5641 and #b39c81 with #191512 shadows and #d1bd9e highlights; accent #1b2330 ≤ 9% of the image on cloth, never walls or spheres; keep #823e25 object bound, never backdrop. Rounded masses taper into cylinders with elliptical openings and curved bands; contours use rounded turns without enclosed gaps; use elevated perspective, overlap, 26.66% high objects, top space, crops, wood, background blur, without graphic additions, rim light, or bloom, unless the scene specifies its own backdrop.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid clean vector illustration, cel animation, glossy 3D rendering, watercolor illustration, ink outlines, flat cel shading, uniformly polished surfaces, copied still life arrangements, copied window layouts, readable text, logos, geometric hatching, tiled patterns, wet floor reflections, floating glitter, and speed lines.

## SCENE
Six still life objects sit on a table in an interior with a window. A sphere and fruit are in front; a metal cup and glass are behind. A potted plant stands behind as cloth spreads from the centre toward the left; the view looks down from above table height, with no people.

## PROBE_ANCHORS
Use elevated perspective. Draw no outlines; light and reflection create edges. Rounded masses taper into cylinders with elliptical openings and curved bands. Use continuous gradients, one connected soft shadow, and soft #1e1207 contact shadows down right. Daylight luminance is about 39.97%, with 16.10% near black but no dominance and 9.5% bright planes; night keeps roles darker. Use #6d5641 and #b39c81, #191512 shadows, and #d1bd9e highlights; accent #1b2330 ≤ 9% of the image on cloth, not walls or matte spheres; #823e25 stays object bound. Keep worn wood ground, objects about 26.66% high, and upper breathing room, unless the scene specifies its own backdrop. Detail budget: sparse, with about four edges, about six compressed grooves, no hatching, protrusions, decorations, or patterns.
