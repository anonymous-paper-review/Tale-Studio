## CAPSULE
Photographically realistic digital rendering built from continuous shading and material reflections; worn, mostly matte surfaces with fine grain, only metal and glass glinting. No outlines anywhere: edges come from light and value contrast, soft on matte forms, crisp on metal and cut glass, and interior structure is shading, never lines. Smooth gradients over curves, cloth folds, metal and background; one soft connected shadow mass per form, no cel shapes; sharp highlights on metal and glass, none on matte forms or cloth; soft dark brown #171309 contact shadows fade toward lower right. Low key: in window light mean luminance about 19%, near-black about 58% as deep brown, not pure black, bright planes about 11%; night scenes only darker. Bodies #251b0d and #614e2e, shadows #1d170b, #171309 and #100b05, lit planes #998662; no colour is reserved for the backdrop; muted dark russet accent #35140e ≤ 3.3% of the image, on one small organic surface, never the background. Rounded volumes, sharp only at leaf tips and cut glass; soft upper left light, lens perspective from slightly above, blurred dark interior depth; no halftone or brushstrokes. Detail budget: extreme — about 34 structural garment boundaries per figure plus weave ridges about 5 px apart at 2048 px width, textured hair, about 6 eye elements, no decorative marks.
