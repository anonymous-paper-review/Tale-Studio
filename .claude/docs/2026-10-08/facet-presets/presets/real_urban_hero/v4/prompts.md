## CAPSULE
Digital realistic rendering with a photographic look: continuous tone, fine material grain, mirror metal and refracting clear glass, no brush marks. No drawn lines, outlines 0% of image width; forms part by value edges, hard on metal and glass, soft on skin, hair and cloth; seams are shading, about 0.1% of image width, never ink, no hatching. Gradients on spheres, folds, metal and walls shift value and color temperature together; low key in window light, mean luminance about 31%, near-black about 28%, not a mostly black image; night scenes keep the same palette roles, only darker. Soft form shadow, one region per form; hard highlights only on metal, glass rims and fruit, none on cloth; directional cast shadows #243032, contacts #010103, sharp at contact, softening with distance. Palette #75837d, #305462, shadows #140607 and #1a262b, light #acb3a9, none reserved for background; accent saturated red #9f1b1a ≤ 22% of the image, one large cloth plane and its reflection, never dots or matte neutrals. Shapes are spheres, cylinders and thin pointed leaves, with continuous curves, acute leaf tips and right-angled frames, under lens perspective and upper-left sun. Detail budget: maximal, about 43 shaded garment boundaries per figure, hair grain in about 6 clumps, no decorative marks.

## NEGATIVE
Avoid semi-realistic anime, stylized cartoon realism, doll photography, painterly realism, ink outlines, hard cel shading, oversized anime eyes, compressed cartoon torsos, speed lines, decorative bokeh fields, halftone overlays, geometric hatching, ornamental iris rings, screen moire, scan borders, compression blocks, readable text, logos, watermarks, copied still-life arrangement, duplicated wardrobe, copied personal accessories.
