Camera choreography, one move: this shot has exactly one camera move — a slow dolly in that pushes straight toward the girl and the dented tin can she is holding. The camera starts at the framing of the start image, standing off far enough to hold her head, her shoulders and the whole can, then travels forward along its own axis at a slow, even rate so the framing grows steadily tighter, until by the final frame the can and her hands fill noticeably more of the frame than they did at the start. First the framing is the wide one of the start image; then the push forward begins, and while it runs she brushes the dust off the can and exhales. Once the push has begun nothing else changes — no pan, no tilt, no lateral track, no orbit, no crane, no change of height or roll, and the push never stops or reverses — and it continues unbroken through the final frame.
