2 Who we share data with

  • Google: sign-in when you choose Google. Google confirms your identity to us and provides your basic profile fields; Google's own handling of your Google account is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ, Estonia): content-policy screening for text prompts before image or video generation, including free generations. Creem's handling of data is described in its Privacy Notice.
  • KG Inicis and PortOne (Republic of Korea): card payment processing, refunds, and recurring billing. They receive the buyer name, mobile number, email, and the order and payment information needed to complete the purchase.
  • AI model providers, which run the generation you request: fal for image and video, and Google and Anthropic for the text our agents generate. See §7 for their legal entities, countries, and retention terms.
  • Vercel: application hosting and content delivery; processes requests to the site, including IP addresses and request logs.
  • Supabase: database, authentication session storage, and file storage for your projects and generated output. Your data is stored in Supabase's Singapore region.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where legally compelled.

We do not otherwise disclose your data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Content screening. Before a user-supplied text prompt reaches an image or video generation model, including during free use, we send the prompt to Creem's Moderation API for a content-policy decision. We may also send an internal user or generation reference to associate the decision with the request. We use the decision to allow or block generation. A blocked request, or a request we cannot screen because of an API error, is not sent to an image or video model and does not consume a Take or plan-included generation allowance. See §7 for this transfer.

3 Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or a similar framework applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (creating your account, running generations you request, billing); legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, and debugging); consent where we ask for it separately; and legal obligation for the statutory retention listed above.

4 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, export it in a portable format, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent you previously gave.

To exercise any of these, email support@talestudio.art. We respond within the time applicable law requires for the right you exercise and where you live. You will not be charged, and we will not degrade your service for asking.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handled your request, you can complain to your data protection authority: in Korea, the Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC); in the EEA or UK, your national supervisory authority.

5 Children and supervised users

Accounts are held by adults (see the Terms of Service). We do not create accounts directly for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from a child who has signed up on their own. If we learn that has happened, we delete the account and its data.

Where an organisation, parent, or guardian holds an account and allows a supervised user under 18 to work on it, that account holder is responsible for obtaining whatever parental or institutional consent their own law requires, and for deciding what personal data those users enter. We process that data on the account holder's instructions.

If you believe a child has created an account directly, contact us at support@talestudio.art.

6 Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Google sign-in is authenticated through Google. If email-and-password sign-in is made available, authentication is handled through Supabase; we do not need your plaintext password to provide the Service.
  • Payment card data does not reach us. Card entry and payment processing are handled by KG Inicis through PortOne; full card details do not reach us.
  • Encryption in transit, and access controls limiting who can reach production data.
  • Data at rest is stored with Supabase, on enterprise-grade infrastructure with encryption at rest. The application itself runs on Vercel.
  • Your project data and files are stored in Supabase's Singapore region. See §7 for the full cross-border picture.

No system is 100% secure. We use authentication providers for sign-in Creem for prompt screening, and KG Inicis through PortOne for card processing.
